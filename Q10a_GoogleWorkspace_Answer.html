Using Google Workspace as a Cloud Collaboration Tool for a Hybrid National Technical Symposium

Subject: Cloud Computing & Services  ·  Text Explanation + UI Mock-ups + Flow Diagrams
▲ 10 Marks Answer
01Introduction

The question asks us to consider the task of inviting PG students from other states to a Departmental Annual National Level Technical Symposium that can be planned in hybrid mode (both on-campus and online participation).

The best cloud service that can be used as a collaborating tool for this purpose is Google Workspace (formerly G Suite) — a suite of cloud-based productivity and collaboration tools by Google, available free for educational institutions through Google Workspace for Education.

It covers all needs: communication, scheduling, registration, file sharing, video conferencing, and live streaming — all in one integrated cloud environment.

Conclusion

Google Workspace is an ideal cloud collaboration platform for organizing a hybrid national-level technical symposium. It is free for educational institutions and requires no special software installation — everything works in a browser.

Google Forms handles registrations from PG students across states, Google Calendar keeps all organizers and participants aligned on the schedule, Google Meet enables real-time virtual participation with screen sharing and Q&A, YouTube Live broadcasts the inauguration to hundreds of remote viewers, and Google Drive centralizes all files — from abstracts to recordings to certificates.

Since all tools are tightly integrated under a single Google account, the symposium can be managed end-to-end on the cloud — making it accessible, scalable, and cost-effective for any department.
